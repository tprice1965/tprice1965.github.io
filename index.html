Nest Peak Guard

Nest Peak Guard is a small personal home-energy tool. It runs on one computer in the owner's home and helps manage electricity use during the local electric cooperative's peak-demand periods.

What it does

  • Reads the electric cooperative's public peak-demand forecast.
  • Switches the owner's own equipment off during forecast peak periods and back on afterwards.
  • Records the temperature, humidity, mode, setpoint and heating status of the owner's own Google Nest thermostats every few minutes, so the owner can see how the building responds when equipment is switched off.

How it uses Google data

Nest Peak Guard uses the Google Smart Device Management API to read thermostat information from the owner's own Google account. It does not change thermostat settings, and it does not share any data with anyone. It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public.
